# YapKitUI

Native SwiftUI views for YapKit conversations.

## Overview

Use ``YapInboxView`` and ``YapConversationView`` for a complete chat surface,
or provide typed renderers through ``YapUIConfiguration`` when your app needs
its own message, composer, header, or state presentation.

The UI layer is host-owned at the navigation boundary: your app decides where
conversations appear and how authenticated identity is presented.
